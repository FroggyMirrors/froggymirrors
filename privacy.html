Froggy Mirrors Bath and Tile ("Froggy Mirrors," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Effortless Upgrade Remodeling LLC, a Tennessee company. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, respond to our ads, or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

By using our website or sending us your information, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a form, respond to one of our ads, call, text, email, or message us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • The address of the project
  • Details about your project, and any photos you choose to send
  • Anything else you choose to share with us

Information from advertising platforms. If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares the information you entered with us.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we and our partners may collect basic technical information, such as your browser and device type, IP address, general location, pages visited, and how you arrived at our site. This is collected through cookies, pixels (such as the Meta Pixel), and similar tools.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To prepare estimates, schedule visits, and complete your project
  • To send invoices, updates, and warranty information
  • To follow up about your project or let you know about our services
  • To understand how our website and ads perform, and to show relevant ads
  • To meet legal requirements and protect our rights

Calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your request or project. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop text messages. We do not share your mobile number or text message consent with third parties for their own marketing.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it as needed to run our business:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as scheduling and customer management software, website hosting, email, and payment processing. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Financing partners, only if you choose to apply for financing.
  • Advertising partners such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to measure and improve our ads.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, our customers, or the public.
  • Business transfers, if our business is sold or merged.

Project photos

We may photograph our work to show in our marketing. We never show names, addresses, or other details that identify you or your home. You can ask us not to use photos of your project.

Cookies and ad choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can manage the ads you see on Facebook and Instagram in your Meta ad preferences. You can also learn more about interest-based advertising and opt out at optout.aboutads.info.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information as long as we need it to serve you, honor our warranty, keep business and tax records, and meet legal requirements. After that, we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what information we have about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Ask us to delete your information, unless we are required to keep it
  • Stop receiving marketing calls, texts, or emails

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

Our website may link to other sites, such as our social media pages. Their privacy practices are their own, so please review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
